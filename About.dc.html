About Radiant Detective Agency

Radiant Detective Agency stands as a trusted name in the field of private investigation, known for its professionalism, precision, and unwavering commitment to truth. Since day one in 2006, we have built a reputation for delivering clear, actionable results through ethical investigation methods and advanced surveillance techniques.

Our work serves individuals as well as businesses: families verifying a prospective match, employers screening candidates, companies conducting due diligence, and banks tracing untraceable defaulters. Every assignment is handled discreetly, documented carefully, and reported in a form our clients can act on.

How we work

Surveillance

Physical shadowing (tailing) the subject to document their daily routine and meetings.

Digital Footprints

Checking social media, online activities, and public records.

Discreet Inquiries

Interviewing neighbors, colleagues, or friends of friends without revealing our or the client's true identity.

Sting Operations

In complex cases, we may use undercover tactics to reveal the truth.
